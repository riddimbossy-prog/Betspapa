# What to expect after v1.18.4

- The navigation item previously called Boss Picks is now Athena.
- Athena may publish fewer matches because RC1 returns NO PICK below 80/100.
- Strong lead, late-separation and multi-route classifications commonly favour Win Either Half, DNB or Double Chance.
- Draw-lock and false-over classifications favour under or draw-related markets.
- High-event or swing classifications may favour Over 1.5 or Over 2.5.
- Directional calls that conflict with bookmaker direction require matching home/away venue confirmation.
- Scores are rule-strength values, not calibrated probabilities.
- Existing PapaSense, Bankers, live fixtures and results are unchanged.
