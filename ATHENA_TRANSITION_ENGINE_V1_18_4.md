# BetsPapa v1.18.4 — Athena Transition Engine

Boss Picks has been retired and replaced by Athena Transition Engine v1.0-RC1.

## Integration

- Original Athena RC1 scoring thresholds remain frozen.
- BetsPapa maps completed fixture history into the nine team-perspective HT/FT states.
- Overall history creates the primary analysis.
- Relevant home and away venue splits are calculated separately.
- Conflicted directional selections require the venue classification to confirm the same side.
- Athena returns one market or NO PICK.
- All supported Athena markets settle from confirmed half-time and full-time scores.
- The day-ahead workflow warms Athena after the main boards are prepared.
