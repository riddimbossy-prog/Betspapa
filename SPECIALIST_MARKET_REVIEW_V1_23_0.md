# Specialist Market Review — v1.23.0

## Markets with the same structural risk as a named team scoring in the second half

The review found seven nearby markets that could be overstated when a blended average hid weak venue or recent evidence:

| Market | Previous risk | v1.23.0 correction |
|---|---|---|
| Team to score in second half | Borderline overall rates and a broad swing bonus could identify the wrong team | Overall + venue + recent floors, direct transition routes, goal-volume gate and an eight-point edge over neutral 2H O0.5 |
| Second-half DNB | A general post-break direction could be mistaken for a repeatable second-half result | Overall + venue + recent win-rate floor and correct venue minimum |
| Second Half Over 0.5 | Strong blended activity could hide a weak sample | Weakest-sample floor across both teams and all three scopes |
| Second Half Over 1.5 | Volatility could overstate two-goal second halves | Repeatable 2H O1.5 rates plus second-half volume confirmation |
| Goals in Both Halves | A high total-goal match could be mistaken for goals in each half | Both-halves history plus independent 1H and 2H goal-leg confirmation |
| First Half Over 0.5 | Early direction could fire without consistent first-half goal history | Conservative six-sample floor and complete half-time data |
| Team Over 0.5 | Team direction could stand in for direct scoring evidence | Team scoring and opponent conceding across overall, venue and recent samples |

## Markets not changed by this specialist review

Broad result and total-goal markets retain their v1.21 PapaSense v2 contracts, HT/FT gates, calibration, contradiction checks and NO PICK behaviour. They still benefit from the new league-only evidence boundary.

## Important distinction

A neutral market can remain valid when a named-team market is rejected. For example, Athena may conclude that a second-half goal is likely but that the data does not clearly identify which team will score it. In that case:

- **Second Half Over 0.5** may qualify.
- **Home/Away team to score in the second half** is withheld.

This prevents the engine from being right about the timing but wrong about the scorer.
